# Tap to Flip — Custom Flip Key, Including Command

Make reviewing a little more comfortable with your own flip key. Tap to Flip lets you choose a single key to reveal a card's answer—including **Command (⌘) on its own on macOS**.

Open **Tools → Tap to Flip Settings…**, click **Choose key…**, press and release your preferred key, and save. Your chosen key reveals the answer when released. Command combinations such as ⌘+C still work normally, and tapping Command on the answer side won't grade the card.

Built for Anki desktop on macOS with Qt 6. Tested on Anki 25.09.4. Restart Anki after installing.
