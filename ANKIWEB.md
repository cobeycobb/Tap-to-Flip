# Tap to Flip

Choose a key to reveal answers in Anki—including **Command (⌘) alone on macOS**.

## How to use

1. Install the add-on and restart Anki.
2. Open **Tools → Tap to Flip Settings…**.
3. Click **Choose key…**, press your preferred key, and click **Save**.
4. While reviewing, tap and release that key to reveal the answer.

Command is selected by default. Shortcuts such as ⌘+C still work, and tapping Command won't grade the card.

Tested on Anki 25.09.4 for macOS.
